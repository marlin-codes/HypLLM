# Proposed HypLoRA research-use license

**DRAFT FOR REVIEW — NOT EFFECTIVE; NO PRESENT GRANT.**

This text is proposed for later express adoption under [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md).
The wording below describes the intended terms after adoption only. Publication,
download, use of this draft branch, PR approval, or merge of this draft does not
activate the proposed permission. No effective date or covered material has been
approved in this document.

## 1. Licensor and covered material

The proposed licensor is the individual using the public alias **marlin-codes**,
solely to the extent that individual owns or is authorized to license the rights
at issue. This is not a grant from Hugging Face, Microsoft, Meta, other upstream
authors, dataset providers, model providers, or the paper's authors collectively.

"Covered Material" would mean only source code and expressly identified related
documentation listed in an approved scope schedule, with exact revisions and any
separable contribution boundaries. Repository location or lack of a header does
not establish inclusion. **The draft scope schedule is empty.** The
[inventory](PROVENANCE.md) records evidence and candidates for review, not a grant.

Third-party material, existing permissively licensed contributions, and all of
`peft/` as currently published are excluded. Data, figures, model weights,
adapters/checkpoints, and outputs are also excluded. This text does not determine
their ownership or licensing merely because the software uses or produces them.

## 2. Proposed free copyright permission

Upon express adoption for an approved scope, the licensor would grant a
non-exclusive, no-charge copyright license to use, reproduce, modify, and share
the Covered Material, including modifications, for:

- Academic research, evaluation, and reproducibility.
- Company-internal research and evaluation without external services or customer
  operational delivery, including experiments assessing possible future
  commercial use.
- Sharing source or executable copies and modifications for these permitted
  research, evaluation, and reproducibility purposes.

A recipient's organization type, research funding, or paid employment alone does
not exclude these uses. Publishing research findings is permitted; the license
does not require publication of findings or modifications. Reuse of independent
outputs is not automatically governed by this code license. Copies of protected
code embedded in an output remain subject to the applicable code permissions.

When sharing Covered Material under these proposed terms, retain the applicable
copyright and license notices and include these terms and the approved scope
schedule so recipients can identify the permission and its limits. Preserve
upstream notices and meet the applicable upstream license conditions separately.

## 3. Commercial permission

The proposed free permission would not cover commercial production deployment
(including internal operational deployment), delivery for customer operational
use, or commercial services using Covered Material. Those activities would require
a separate agreement with the licensor **only for rights not already available
under another applicable license or permission**.

Research sharing allowed by section 2 is not customer operational delivery. A
company's internal research and evaluation remains free when it does not operate
as production or provide external services. No commercial price, automatic
purchase obligation, or standard commercial grant is established here; see the
[commercial outline](COMMERCIAL-AGREEMENT-DRAFT.md).

## 4. Existing grants take precedence for their material

Nothing in the proposed terms cancels or limits an earlier valid license, adds
conditions to exercising upstream rights, or requires payment for use already
permitted by Apache-2.0, MIT, or another existing grant. Previously distributed
copies and modifications must be assessed under the permissions that accompanied
them. Moving code to another file or removing an old header would not erase an
earlier grant. No assumption is made that the licensor can exclusively control
commercial use of the current HypLoRA implementation.

## 5. Matters left for full-text review

These are proposed copyright-use terms, not a completed legal instrument. Before
adoption, the licensor must approve the scope and effective date and review legal
identity/contact arrangements, duration, termination, warranty/liability treatment,
and applicable law/dispute provisions. This draft selects none of those terms,
makes no new patent commitment, and contains no indemnity promise. It does not
alter any existing upstream patent grant, warranty disclaimer, or liability term.
Any additions require express review; this draft supplies no default boilerplate
for unresolved matters.
