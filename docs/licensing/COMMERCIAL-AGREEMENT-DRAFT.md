# Commercial agreement outline — not an agreement

**DRAFT FOR REVIEW — NOT EFFECTIVE; NO PRESENT OFFER OR GRANT.**

This outline records items for a separately negotiated agreement. Publishing or
merging it does not conclude an agreement or authorize a use. The free research
boundary in [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) includes company-internal research
and evaluation without external services or customer operational delivery.

An agreement would address commercial production deployment, customer operational
delivery, or commercial services only for specifically identified material and
rights that the individual licensor can license and that are not already available
to the recipient under an applicable existing grant. It cannot remove upstream
permissions or convert already granted Apache/MIT rights into a paid entitlement.
